# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Panelize to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others’ private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this code, and may restrict participation. To report a problem, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/jayzizzle/panelize/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/jayzizzle). Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
